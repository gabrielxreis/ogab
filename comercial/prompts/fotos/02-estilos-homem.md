# Estilos de foto de perfil

Cada estilo vai assim: o bloco de identidade (`00-identidade.md`) + o texto do estilo, anexando a ficha de personagem aprovada e 1 ou 2 fotos reais. Onde aparecer man, trocar por man ou woman. Para mulher, troque a roupa dos estilos 3 e 4 pelo que está em "versão feminina".

## 1. Cinema quente (fundo carvão)
Ultra realistic cinematic portrait, chest-up framing, man sitting naturally with shoulders relaxed while looking slightly away from the camera, neutral expression, eye-level angle, dark charcoal studio background, dramatic Rembrandt lighting combined with subtle warm rim light creating depth, luxury editorial photography, natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, captured on a full-frame camera using an 85mm portrait lens.

## 2. Editorial luxo (fundo preto)
Highly realistic luxury portrait, chest-up composition, man standing naturally with relaxed shoulders and arms outside the frame, neutral expression, slight three-quarter angle, solid black background with a soft gradient, dramatic split lighting using a focused key light balanced by subtle fill light and a clean rim light, premium fashion editorial photography, ultra detailed skin texture, shallow depth of field, captured with a full-frame camera and an 85mm portrait lens.

## 3. Filme 35 mm no sofá vintage (P&B)
A high-contrast black and white medium-shot portrait of a dapper man sitting on a vintage patterned sofa. The subject is posing with legs crossed and one arm draped casually over the backrest, looking directly at the camera with a confident, intense gaze. Wearing a tailored light-colored blazer, a white dress shirt, a dark tie with a silver tie clip, and dark contrasting trousers. The background features a symmetrical wall. The lighting is soft, cinematic studio lighting that sculpts the face, rendered in the style of classic 35mm film photography with fine grain, sharp focus, and a timeless, elegant aesthetic. Aspect ratio 2:3, 4K resolution.

## 4. Estúdio minimalista na cadeira (P&B)
Edit this image of a man into a black and white artistic portrait in a minimalist fashion studio. Dressed in a tailored dark suit with sharp lines, paired with polished black shoes. Sitting on a simple modern chair, leaning slightly forward with hands clasped, giving an introspective and confident expression. Vertical 4:5 crop, cinematic editorial style: refined, timeless, and powerful.

## 5. Vista de cima (grande-angular)
A striking, ultra-wide-angle overhead shot captures this man from a dramatic top-down perspective, creating an isolating effect against a smooth gradient grey studio background that transitions from darker at the edges to lighter in the center, directly under the subject. The camera is positioned about 90 degrees directly above the subject, framing the entire body with emphasis on the head and upper torso, with ample negative space around. Camera settings: ISO 200, f/2.8, 1/200s. Keep the subject's own hair, glasses (if any) and features exactly as in the reference. Attire: a short-sleeved button-up shirt in a rich dark brown corduroy or textured fabric, worn open over a light beige or off-white knitted sweater or textured t-shirt. The gaze is directed upwards, directly into the camera, with an engaging, slightly curious expression. Soft, even lighting from above, subtle shadows defining features and clothing folds, natural skin texture without harshness. Minimalist setup, the subject is the sole focal point, high-resolution contemporary portrait.

## 6. Laranja para preto (luz de fundo quente)
Transform this image into a high-end cinematic studio portrait with a smooth orange-to-black gradient backdrop. Introduce a subtle warm orange backlight glow behind the subject, gradually fading into rich black tones to evoke depth and contrast. Apply soft, natural daylight-style white key lighting on the face, enhancing contours without altering the subject's original appearance. Simulate the photographic quality of a Sony Alpha A7 III with a 35mm Helios lens: creamy shallow depth of field and delicate vintage-style bokeh around the body and background. Do not alter any facial features: face, hair, skin texture, eyes, nose and lips remain entirely untouched. The transformation focuses only on lighting, atmosphere and image quality, creating a cinematic, gallery-grade portrait.

## 7. Longa exposição (rastro de movimento)
Vibe: kinetic, artistic and cinematic. Effect: long exposure motion blur (shutter drag); the subject's movement creates a ghostly, soft smearing effect around the edges of the silhouette while the face stays sharp and recognizable. Lighting: continuous warm amber tungsten light against a dark moody background. Styling: minimalist dark clothing that flows with the movement. Expression: a calm, steady gaze looking through the motion blur. Tech: 35mm lens, slow shutter speed (1/15s), visible film grain, rich textures, sophisticated motion trails.

## 10. Sombra diagonal (P&B, só para capa ou post: esconde metade do rosto)

The head sits about two thirds up from the bottom, horizontally centered, on a smooth light-grey background. A sharp shadow cuts the image diagonally, hiding the lower half of the face and the neck, leaving the eyes, eyebrows and upper forehead visible and emphasizing the facial contours. Keep his real hair, eyes and features exactly as in the reference. Skin above the shadow line rendered as a smooth gradient from mid grey to light grey with a matte texture. Black clothing, likely a high collar, merges with the shadow and creates a silhouette effect. Hard lighting from the upper left casting strong, deep black shadows. Eye-level angle, shallow depth of field, post-processing for extra contrast and sharpness.
